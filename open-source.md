  <h2 id="texstage">texstage</h2>
  <p>texstage adds live quizzes and collaborative activities to LaTeX Beamer slides. Write questions alongside your lecture in the same <code>.tex</code> file, present in the browser, and let students participate from their phones without an app or account.</p>
  <p class="muted small">Open source under the Apache License 2.0.</p>
